Real CSVs in the original attack corpus

2026-10-19. Two things are checked here: whether the real-dataset charts (PR #8) match the CSVs in datasets/, and how the original attack corpus was moved from the dummy datasets onto the CSVs, with the attacks that still need adjustment.

Terms

  • Original corpus: the hand-written chart pages and attacks (clean_<type>.html, attack_<type>_<attack>.html) and the copies build_dataset_pages.py generates from them for each dataset.
  • Real-dataset pages: the pages from PR #8 (build_real_dataset_pages.py, datasets/real_datasets.json, tasks/extended_tasks.json).
  • Dummy datasets: the placeholder entries dummy1-dummy3 in datasets/dummy_datasets.json, used by the original corpus until now.
  • Core datasets: the new entries climate_core, public_health_core and steam_core in datasets/corpus_datasets.json, computed from the CSVs by datasets/build_corpus_datasets.py.
  • Shape rule: a property of a chart’s numbers that the original corpus’s task answers or attack premises depend on (for example “series 2 rises with exactly one dip”); build_dataset_pages.py refuses a dataset that breaks one.
  • TM / MT: the attack tiers. TM (truthful to the human, misleading to the agent) hides false claims in content only the agent reads; MT (misleading to the human) changes what a person sees, such as axes or colors.
  • CDC CDI: CDC Chronic Disease Indicators, the source of public_health.csv.

CSV check of the real-dataset charts

Terms: “reproduces” means datasets/build_real_datasets.py recomputes the chart’s numbers from the CSV and they equal the numbers in real_datasets.json that the pages were built from.

All 12 real-dataset charts (3 datasets x bar, line, scatter, stacked bar) reproduce from the CSVs. One error was fixed: the public health scatter counted the “United States” national row as a state. It is removed; no task answer changes (r = 0.889, Alaska is still the only state where the female rate exceeds the male rate, the highest point is still 50.4, 28.1).

Choices that are not wrong but should be known when reading results:

Chart Choice
public health, all “States” means the 10 (bar) or 5 (line, stacked) most populous states, with population derived as deaths / crude rate x 100,000, since the CSV has no population column. public_health.csv is a 50,000-row sample of CDC CDI; the liver-disease rows used are complete.
steam bar A game counts only toward its first listed genre, and only 10 hand-picked genres are shown. Averaging over every listed genre gives very different prices (Racing 14.30 vs 6.85).
steam stacked bar Review tiers use cut-offs of 80 / 60 / 40 % positive, not Steam’s own rating bands.
steam scatter The 300-game sample’s seed is unknown and no seed reproduces it, so its app ids are pinned in datasets/steam_scatter_sample.json.
climate stacked bar “Other industry” is left out and land-use change is included; land-use change is negative in 3 years, so those stacks dip below zero.

Analysis: the charts are faithful to the CSVs; the steam genre and tier choices are the ones most likely to be questioned by a reader.

Two generator fixes came with this. A new shape rule requires the two lines to peak in different years, because line_close_colors uses the second line’s peak as its bait (India vs Indonesia peaked in the same year, so bait and answer were equal). Scatter charts whose x values are years now print them without a thousands separator, and the Vega-Lite version no longer starts the x axis at zero.

Analysis: the corpus now has 8 real datasets in place of 12 dummy ones, so each library has 29 original-corpus attack pages on real data instead of 42 on dummy data.

Terms: “no fit” means no view of that CSV meets the shape rules, so the attack cannot be copied onto that dataset without changing the attack or its task.

Analysis: 13 attack x dataset combinations have no real fit (10 for public health, 3 for steam), and the 3 reasoning attacks stay on dummy data; all other original-corpus attacks now run on real numbers.
